Shipping Policy

Shipping policy for Dental Factory, operated by Bharti Dent India.

Business details

Dental Factory is the website and trading brand used for dental supply enquiries. The legal business name for billing, GST, and KYC records is Bharti Dent India.

Website: https://dental-factory.onrender.com

Dispatch

Most in-stock consumables dispatch within 24-48 business hours after order confirmation and payment/callback verification. Equipment orders may need stock, freight, and installation confirmation before dispatch.

Delivery

Delivery timelines depend on city, product size, courier availability, and clinic address serviceability. Metro consumable orders usually move faster; bulky equipment can take longer because freight and installation slots may be required.

Charges

Shipping charges, if applicable, are shared before final confirmation. Bulky equipment may have separate freight or installation charges.

Invoice and GST

Invoices are issued under Bharti Dent India where applicable. GST details are shared on the final invoice according to the confirmed order and billing details.
