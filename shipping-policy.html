Dispatch

Most in-stock consumables dispatch within 24-48 business hours after order confirmation and payment/callback verification. Equipment orders may need stock, freight, and installation confirmation before dispatch.

Delivery

Delivery timelines depend on city, product size, courier availability, and clinic address serviceability. Metro consumable orders usually move faster; bulky equipment can take longer because freight and installation slots may be required.

Charges

Standard shipping is Rs. 99 for eligible consumable orders below Rs. 2,999. Eligible consumable orders of Rs. 2,999 or more qualify for free shipping.

Bulky equipment, clinic setup items, high-value orders, and installation-linked deliveries may require separate freight or installation confirmation during callback before dispatch.

Cash on delivery and card swipe

Cash on delivery is available only for standard eligible orders up to Rs. 20,000. COD may be disabled for equipment, bulky freight, clinic setup, high-value, or non-serviceable locations.

Card swipe on delivery through Pine Labs, UPI on delivery, bank transfer, or online payment may be offered after order confirmation where available.

Invoice and GST

Invoices are issued under Bharti Dent India where applicable. GSTIN 07AYHPS5357F1Z3 is used on eligible GST invoices according to the confirmed order and billing details.
