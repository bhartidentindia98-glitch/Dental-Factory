# Dental Factory Brand Guide

## Brand Name

Dental Factory

## Positioning

Dental Factory is a practical dental ecommerce and clinic procurement platform for dentists, students, dealers, and new clinic owners.

## Tagline

Clinic supplies, factory-direct confidence.

## Short Description

Dental Factory helps clinics buy dental instruments, materials, equipment, consumables, and setup bundles from one focused marketplace.

## Visual Direction

- Primary: deep dental teal for trust, action, and navigation.
- Secondary: graphite ink for professional text and admin surfaces.
- Accent: clinical coral and warm gold for offers, urgency, and highlights.
- Radius: 8px, clean and operational rather than playful.
- Icon style: lucide line icons, with a factory mark for brand recognition.

## Voice

Clear, procurement-friendly, and confident. Use direct phrases like:

- Same day dispatch on clinic essentials
- Factory-direct confidence for dental supplies
- Build your clinic basket faster
- Procurement support for equipment and consumables

Avoid copying Dentalkart wording or branding.
